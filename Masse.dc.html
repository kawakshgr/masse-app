scope What ships first Everything above is designed. Not all of it should ship at once. The cut rule: v1 is the loop a coach and a client run every week — invite, train, log, check in, adjust. Anything that needs its own data model, its own pricing, or a season of history to be trustworthy waits.
phase 1 v1 — ship it The weekly loop, end to end. Nothing here is optional: remove one and the product stops working.
Onboarding Invite code to a written week 1. The front door — nothing downstream exists without it.
Today + Train Log sets, rest timer, Live Activity. This is the screen they open six days a week.
Weekly check-in Weight, measurements, photos, three questions, one form clip. What clients are actually paying for.
Program builder Drag exercises into a week, save it as a template. The coach’s one irreplaceable tool.
Roster Who needs attention today, ordered by signal rather than alphabet.
Chat With attachments and voice notes, so it replaces the WhatsApp thread instead of sitting beside it.
Hevy import A one-time history pull. Cheap to build and it makes week 1 credible on day one.
phase 2 Soon after Real value, but the loop survives a few weeks without them. Ship when v1 is stable.
History & weekly review Strength over months, records, photo compare, and the note the coach sends. Every number is read off logged sets, so it needs a season before it says anything.
Cycle tracking Phase and private symptoms only — tracking without the automatic load math.
Health / Health Connect Read-only sleep and steps. Removes the two fields people lie about.
iPad The same screens, unstacked. No new surface to design, so it is mostly layout work.
Billing Invoices and open balances. Coaches will chase payment by hand for a month without complaining.
later Each is its own product Not cut for lack of value — cut because each one carries a data model, a pricing question, or a trust problem of its own.
Phase-aware auto-scaling Needs a season of real training data before the math deserves anyone’s trust. Ship tracking first, learn, then automate.
Nutrition & macro coaching A food database, a plan builder and a compliance model. That is a second app wearing the same colours.
Video review room Scrub, draw, voice-over. Build it when clients have asked twice — form clips in chat cover most of it.
Native Mac app The web app already does this job. Revisit when seat counts make it worth a second codebase.
